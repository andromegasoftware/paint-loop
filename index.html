Prototype for feel testing. Tap anywhere to start the music. Levels 1 to 3 have no gates. Levels 4 to 6 add color gates: red + yellow = orange, blue + yellow = green, red + blue = purple. Scoring: 10 per pixel; quick snaps in a row build a combo up to x5; pixels eaten after a color change score double; empty branch spots and unused chameleons add a bonus at the end.
